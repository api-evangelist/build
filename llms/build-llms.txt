# Build

> Build is an AI-native operating partner for the built world, combining autonomous AI agents (its "Dougie" platform) with domain expertise to accelerate commercial real estate and infrastructure development. It automates mission-critical development workflows — site selection, due diligence, underwriting, investment-committee memos, test-fit analysis, market and zoning research, environmental diligence, and ESG reporting — for institutional clients across data centers, industrials, energy, power, office, telecommunications, mixed-use, hotels, and multifamily.
